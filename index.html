Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 May 2025

Breathe Relief (“the App”) values your privacy. This page explains what data we (or rather, do not) collect when you use the App.

1. No Personal Data Collected

The App is fully functional without an account and does not request any personal information such as your name, email, health data or location.

2. On‑Device Only Statistics

The App stores basic session statistics (number of sessions, total minutes and streak count) locally on your device so you can track your progress. These statistics never leave your device and are deleted automatically if you uninstall the App.

3. No Third‑Party SDKs

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not share data with any third parties.

4. Crash & Diagnostics

We do not collect crash logs by default. If in the future we add an optional crash reporting feature, it will be opt‑in and documented here before release.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is rated 4+ on the App Store and is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App’s release notes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please email privacy@yourdomain.com.

Thank you for using Breathe Relief.
