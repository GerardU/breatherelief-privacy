Breathe Relief Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Breathe Relief is a breathing and general wellness app for iPhone and iPad. No account is required. We do not show advertisements, sell wellbeing data, or use advertising identifiers to track you across apps.

Your breathing history stays on your device

Session history, subjective before-and-after check-ins, preferences, favourites, personal routines, reminder schedules and recommendations are stored locally. We do not send your raw wellbeing scores or breathing history to RevenueCat or an analytics service. Device backups may contain local app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes payments. Breathe Relief uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access and manage Plus and Nature Collection entitlements. RevenueCat processes purchase history, transaction and product identifiers, subscription status, an anonymous app-user identifier, and technical app information needed for these functions. Purchase history also supports subscription reporting in RevenueCat. We do not provide RevenueCat with your name, email address, payment-card details or wellbeing scores.

Apple-signed app acquisition information is checked on your device to protect qualifying paid-upfront customers. It is not a substitute for a local, editable ownership flag. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy for their practices.

Product analytics and diagnostics

The Nature Collection update enables TelemetryDeck for limited product events, such as app opens, session starts and completions, previews, routine saves, reminder interactions and technical purchase outcomes. App version, build, interface language, platform, navigation source, product identifiers, environment and technical result may accompany these events. A random app-local analytics identifier is hashed by the TelemetryDeck SDK before transmission. It is separate from the RevenueCat purchase identifier; we do not join these identifiers to your wellbeing history. We do not send your selected emotional need, scores, subjective results, notes, routine names or routine schedules. These events describe product use, not a medical record or proof of payment. The earlier 7.0.1 release has no TelemetryDeck App ID and does not send these product events. Purchase reporting through RevenueCat is separate. See TelemetryDeck’s privacy policy. Apple may provide crash and diagnostic reports according to your device sharing preferences.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so we can respond. Please do not include wellbeing scores, medical information, passwords or payment-card details. Support email is handled through Gmail.

Your choices and data removal

You can remove local app data by deleting the app; restoring a device backup may restore that data. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat. Use Apple subscription settings to cancel. For questions or requests concerning purchase-related data or support correspondence, contact us below. Some transaction records may need to be retained for purchase verification or legal obligations.

Children and safety

The app does not request names, ages, contacts or location. It is a general wellness tool, not medical care, diagnosis or emergency assistance. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a child may have sent to support.

Policy updates

We will update this page when our data practices change and revise the date above.

Breathe Relief Support

For help with the app, purchases, accessibility, or privacy, email mosartgames@gmail.com. Include the app version, device model and a short description of the problem when useful. Do not include sensitive wellbeing details.

Restore access: use Settings → Restore Purchases in Breathe Relief while signed in to the Apple Account that made the original purchase. Qualifying paid-upfront owners keep permanent Plus access after verification; they do not need to buy a subscription again. If access is missing, contact support before purchasing.

Subscriptions and terms

Downloading Breathe Relief is free. Optional Plus annual and monthly subscriptions unlock the same Plus features while active. Plus permanent is a restorable one-time purchase providing permanent access to Plus features and this app’s own collections. Nature Collection is a separate restorable one-time purchase of Forest, Rain Window and Ocean, including their ambient soundscapes; it does not unlock other Plus features. Those environments remain owned if a Plus subscription later expires. Plus does not automatically discount or credit a previous Nature Collection purchase. The App Store shows the localized price and any eligible trial before you confirm a purchase. A subscription renews automatically unless cancelled in your Apple Account subscription settings at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Manage or cancel through Apple subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. For payment or refund questions, use Apple's purchase support.
